# Implementation notes

Every off-plan decision and change made while implementing the plans in `.plans/plans/`, newest last. One entry each: what, why, where. The plan files keep their own logs; this is the list a reader checks first.

## stats-backend

- **2026-11-07, power mean keeps its sign.** Phase 2's `power_mean` took |x| for every p, so the linear mean of signed values was the mean of magnitudes. set-merge averages signed densities bin by bin, which that would silently break. Now p = 1 and odd integer p keep the sign, an even p is sign-free, any other p > 0 refuses a negative value, p ≤ 0 needs values above zero or a `floor`. `framework/stats/means.py`, tests in `tests/runner/test_stats_means.py`.
- **2026-11-07, log ratio floor.** The plan's ℓ = ln((max(y,0)+ε·ref)/ref) charged an exact curve ln 1.01 in every bin and made `norm` unequal for a curve 1.2× high and 1/1.2 low. Now ℓ = ln(max(y, ε·ref)/ref). `framework/stats/metrics.py`.
- **2026-11-07, default `worst` thresholds (7, 25), not (2, 9).** The mean of the top 4 of 40 squared pulls of a true, equally noisy curve has median 4.1 and p97 6.6, so good = 2 charged a perfect curve about 0.3. The same reasoning set `cvar` (14, 45), `tail` (2.5, 6), `poisson` (3.5, 9). Reasons beside each entry (`Metric.why`).
- **2026-11-07, phase 2 test fixture.** The noisy true curve had a 2 % reference error while the curve was noisy, so pulls were too small and every threshold looked safe. It is now two independent Poisson samples of one truth.
- **2026-11-07, `poisson` Jeffreys floor in weighted units.** The notes floored the curve's content at 0.5 weighted units, right only where each weighted unit is one count; it is 0.5/s, s = unweighted counts per unit of content.
- **2026-11-07, `chi2_smooth` denominator.** The notes' 2·V_n gives 1.5 for a smooth curve with honest errors; the neighbours' interpolated variance plus their mean error² as the bin's own gives about 1 and still lets no spike excuse itself.
- **2026-11-07, barycentre rate** is taken over the distance between bin middles (the notes divided by one bin's width, wrong when widths vary), may be negative, and the last bin takes its neighbour's.
- **2026-11-07, `lambda` is a descriptor** (`better = "none"`, no thresholds), and `r2_ln` is 1 − R² so lower is better; a decay rate has no better or worse.
- **2026-11-07, Hodges–Lehmann refuses unequal weights** instead of ignoring them; refuses more than 1000 values.
- **2026-11-07, Wasserstein is exact for the linear level mean** (levels at every member's CDF breakpoint, both sides of a plateau kept), not the notes' 1000-point grid, which smeared gaps and was 1.5 % off for identical members. The yield of both ensemble means is the linear mean of the members' yields.
- **2026-11-07, means dropped from the catalogue:** circular, EMA, logarithmic and Heronian, per set-merge's plan.
- **2026-11-07, `Metric` gained fields** `why`, `pool`, `loss()`, `scored()` and directions `higher` and `none`; `Mean` gained `kind`, `error_kind`, `settings`.
- **2026-11-07, test data.** The developer named four generator-by-PDF studies in a read-only work dir for tests and examples. They are inputs only, never written to; the repo names none of them (invariants).
- **2026-11-07, phase 4: `statstable` imports `stats.table` at module level.** The rule "runner modules import stats only inside functions" had no room for the plan's "statstable re-exports it for a release".
