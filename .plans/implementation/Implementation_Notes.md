- **2026-11-10 · 3.1.** A `one` metric's thresholds are taken as given on |log₁₀ m|, not oriented again: the registry states `yield_ratio`'s (0.009, 0.065) in that unit, and the schema's `[ranking].thresholds` says "in the metric's scored unit". `lower` and `higher` thresholds are oriented as a value is (`n_eff`'s (1000, 100) → q −3, −2). 03 §1.2's z = (q(m) − q(g)) / (q(b) − q(g)) read literally for `one` would take |log₁₀ 0.009|.
- **2026-11-10 · 3.1.** P3 says "a non-finite q is +∞". Taken for q = +∞ (`one` at m ≤ 0, a lower metric at +∞). A q of −∞ (a `higher` metric at +∞, infinitely good) has excess 0, not +∞: an infinite effective size is not an infinite cost.
