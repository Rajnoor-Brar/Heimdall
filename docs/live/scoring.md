---
id: live-scoring
summary: the statistics summary and the report's statistics by purpose: standings, a section a purpose, a metric's ranking and table, a head to head only when asked
affects: plots, reports
issue: I-120
---

**Now holds:** `statistics/<set>/summary.pdf` (and `summary.html`, `summary/summary_NN.png`) is built by `bars.summary_model(rows, heading, settings)` from the metrics that are on (`[score].metrics`, the five defaults when it names none) and scored by [the engine](score-engine.md). Its front matter (facts, contents, notes) comes first; then **Standings**: a table, ranked within each page cell, of each point's `score`, its `worst page` and `worst` loss, a `veto` column when `[score].veto` is set, a column for each purpose with a metric on (its value, shaded), and a `dominated` column when asked; and a table of the purposes with the metrics on under each. Then one section for each purpose that has a metric on, in the order of `stats.metrics.PURPOSES`: a ranking of the points' value for the purpose (a cost, 1 being its metrics at their bad thresholds), then for each of its metrics a ranking (each point's `[score].mean` over the pages where it was compared, the root mean square by default), the page cells against the curves, and a table (a row per point, a column per object, shaded by the metric's cost, so a colour means the same on every table; a table past bad is shaded up to its own worst). Then **Head to head**, only for the pages `[score].head_to_head` names (`"<cell>/<object>"`, or `"all"`): each pair of the page's curves, the row's loss less the column's, no wins counted. A metric with no number in the rows has no section.

Each note is the registry's: a purpose's is `metrics.ASKS`, a metric's `metrics.describe` (its title, note, thresholds and why they are those); how a page is compared and how a score is made are bars.py's `COMPARED` and `_score_text`. `bars.draw` and a page's image show a panel for each metric on (`bars.panels`, `bars.grid`).

`statistics.json` carries the `[score]` the table was scored with (the errand's, a set's `score` over it), so `heimdall report` scores a source's csv the way its run did. A report's `stats` names `standings`, `purpose:<name>`, `head-to-head`, a metric (`chi2_ndf`: its ranking, page cells against curves and table), or `<metric>:ranking|crossed|table`; the default Standings section names `standings`, every purpose, and `head-to-head` (a purpose with no metric on, and a head to head nobody asked for, are left out without a word). The old names `top5*`, `scoreboard*`, `head-to-head-<n>` and `area` are refused naming `standings` or `head-to-head`. How the numbers are made names each purpose, each metric (a formula for the five old columns, its note for the rest), how points are scored and the head to head.

**No longer holds:** places and points (5, 4, 3, …), wins, win rates and matches counted; the top five and the scoreboards; a head to head for every page cell; the `area` table and panel (the column stays in `statistics.csv`); the fixed six panels and tables; `bars.PANELS`, `TABLES`, `DEFINITIONS`, `NOTES`, `POINTS`, `SCORED`.

**Decisions:** presentation draws only: it reads `stats.score` and the registries. The summary shades a metric by its hinged cost, not by the table's own range, so that two tables are read alike. The page cells against curves table stays (the plan named only ranking and table). A figure's `weight` is not read by the summary yet (a page's row does not say its figure): only `[score].weights` apply there; the engine takes `figure_weights` when a caller has them.

**Stable pages need:** `guide/plots-statistics.md` and `guide/reports.md`, which still describe the top five and the scoreboards; `live/stats-rms.md` is superseded by this page.
